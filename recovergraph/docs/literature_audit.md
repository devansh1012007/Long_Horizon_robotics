## Exit-criterion statement (spec 14): what is and is not new

**Not new (established):**
- Per-subgoal verification and verification-based orchestration for long-horizon manipulation (LADDER's design axes; B4 stand-in).
- Pose/wrench-threshold fault detection with forward/backward recovery (Goller et al. 2025; reproduced as baseline B5).
- LLM-based failure explanation and LLM-guided correction (REFLECT, AHA, unified VLM+BT frameworks).
- Causal models for explaining/predicting robot execution failures (Diehl et al.).
- Belief-space planning under uncertainty before execution (Kaelbling & Lozano-Pérez).
- Rollback/backtracking and retry/fallback recovery primitives (BTs, assembly backtracking).

**Proposed wedge (to be defended, not claimed as established):**
1. A per-step **action-effect ledger** with a chi-square divergence statistic and per-variable-group false-alarm control, feeding a **calibrated Bayesian fault posterior** (with information-gain disambiguation probes) rather than an LLM judgment or a fixed threshold.
2. **Dependency-aware invalidation** through carried-variable edges of a task DAG: precisely marking which downstream subgoals are stale after a diagnosed fault, instead of full replanning (the spec's stated key difference).
3. **Cost-minimising safe recovery selection** over the fault posterior with a hard, independent-safety constraint and a loop guard.
4. A **reproducible, mechanism-labelled 7-fault injection benchmark** (seeded, stratified, compound faults) tied to go/no-go criteria and pre-registered statistics.

Whether (1)-(4) jointly beat strong baselines is exactly what the pre-registered
evaluation (docs/pre_registration.md) must decide; a negative result is an
accepted outcome (spec Section 15 pivot).
